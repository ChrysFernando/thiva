Winrich Customer Service Knowledge Base

About Winrich:
Winrich is a leading home appliances and electronics company based in Sri Lanka. We manufacture and distribute a wide range of household products including rice cookers, blenders, fans, irons, and kitchen appliances.

Product Warranty:
All Winrich products come with a standard 1-year manufacturer warranty. Extended warranty of up to 3 years is available for select premium products. Warranty covers manufacturing defects but does not cover damage from misuse or unauthorized repairs.

Return Policy:
Products can be returned within 7 days of purchase if unused and in original packaging. Defective products can be exchanged within 30 days. For returns, customers need their purchase receipt and original packaging.

Frequently Asked Questions:
Q: How do I claim warranty?
A: Visit any authorized service center with your product, purchase receipt, and warranty card. Our technicians will assess the product and process the warranty claim within 3-5 business days.

Q: Do you deliver island-wide?
A: Yes, Winrich delivers to all areas in Sri Lanka. Delivery within Colombo district is free for orders above LKR 5,000. Other areas have a flat delivery charge of LKR 350.

Q: Can I order replacement parts?
A: Yes, replacement parts are available for all current and recent models. Contact our hotline at 1234 or visit a service center to order parts.
